One launcher. It installs the client, keeps it updated and gets you to the login screen.

Download the launcher

Windows launcher, about 60 MB. Java is bundled, nothing else to install.

Download for Windows Downloads open with the beta
01Run the launcher. It fetches the client and the game cache on first start.
02Create your account on the login screen. Pick a name you'll keep.
03Link your Discord in game with ::link for rewards and your rank role.

Requirements

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  • 4 GB RAM (8 GB recommended for 117 HD)
  • Any GPU from the last ten years for HD rendering; the client also runs without it
